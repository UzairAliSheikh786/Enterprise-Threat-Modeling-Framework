03 / Qualitative risk prioritization

Illustrative ranking; scores are assumptions, not measured organizational risk.

P1 · CRITICAL Privilege escalation (T-06)

Potential for broad control over identity and infrastructure. Validate privilege boundaries and emergency access.

Suggested urgency: immediate validation

P1 · CRITICAL Ransomware / backup impact (T-08)

Potential disruption and recovery failure if production and backups share trust or credentials.

Suggested urgency: immediate validation

P2 · HIGH Identity compromise (T-01)

Credential theft can bypass intended trust assumptions if authentication and sessions are weak.

Suggested urgency: near-term control review

P2 · HIGH Data exposure / tampering (T-02, T-04, T-07)

Authorization gaps, exposed secrets, or weak integrity controls can harm confidentiality and trust.

Suggested urgency: near-term control review
Scoring method: priority is a qualitative judgment based on plausible likelihood and impact for the hypothetical model. No organization-specific likelihood data, asset valuation, or control testing has been supplied. Re-score with stakeholders and evidence before operational use.

04 / Zero Trust control set

Verify explicitly, use least privilege, and assume breach.

01 / Identity

MFA for users and administrators; conditional access; short-lived sessions; rapid revocation; service identity inventory.

02 / Device posture

Managed devices, endpoint detection, encryption, patch compliance, and restricted access from unhealthy devices.

03 / Least privilege

Role-based access, separate admin accounts, just-in-time elevation, quarterly access review, remove dormant accounts.

04 / Network boundaries

Segment workloads, restrict east-west traffic, deny by default, protect administrative interfaces, review firewall rules.

05 / Data protection

Classify sensitive data, encrypt in transit and at rest, limit exports, manage secrets centrally, validate authorization.

06 / Visibility + recovery

Centralize relevant logs, alert on high-risk behavior, protect log integrity, isolate backups, and test restoration.

05 / Incident response blueprint

Adapt the phases, contacts, and decision thresholds to the organization's approved plan.

01 / Preparation

  1. Assign incident commander and technical leads.
  2. Maintain asset, identity, and escalation inventories.
  3. Pre-stage approved containment steps and evidence procedures.
  4. Exercise backups and communication channels.

02 / Detection & analysis

  1. Validate alert context and scope.
  2. Preserve relevant logs and timestamps.
  3. Identify affected identities, hosts, data, and trust boundaries.
  4. Assess severity and notify designated owners.

03 / Containment, eradication & recovery

  1. Use authorized isolation or account-revocation procedures.
  2. Preserve evidence before destructive changes where feasible.
  3. Remove root cause and rotate exposed credentials.
  4. Restore from verified clean backups; monitor closely.

04 / Post-incident

  1. Document timeline, impact, decisions, and evidence.
  2. Complete root-cause and control-gap review.
  3. Track corrective actions with owners and due dates.
  4. Update detections, runbooks, and training.
Operational boundary: This is a planning aid only. It does not connect to systems, scan hosts, collect telemetry, isolate devices, or execute incident response actions. Follow organizational authorization, legal, privacy, and evidence-handling requirements.
